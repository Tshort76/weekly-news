LENS: the architecture of rule, not the contest for it.

Score FIT 0–3 on whether the item describes a change in HOW a system is organized:
its rules, constraints, capacities, incentives, or boundaries.

3 — A structural fact changed or was revealed. Examples: a central bank adopts a new
    operating framework; a country revokes or creates a property regime; a cartel forms
    or breaks; a supply chain reroutes for durable reasons; a state gains or loses a
    capacity (fiscal, military, administrative); a technology shifts the cost of
    coordination or control; a demographic threshold crossed; a market's plumbing
    changes (clearing, settlement, reserve currency share, capital controls).
2 — Meaningful movement inside an existing structure with clear mechanism: a tariff
    regime extended, a large firm restructured, a regulatory rule with second-order
    effects, an industrial policy with real money attached, a court decision that
    changes what institutions can do.
1 — Interesting but mostly contest: an election result, a leadership change, a
    diplomatic spat, a strike — UNLESS the item explains a structural consequence.
0 — Personalities, polls, scandals, tactical maneuvering, culture-war, sport,
    celebrity, human-interest, opinion pieces without a mechanism.

KIND:
  architecture — the item is about how things are organized (rules, systems, capacity)
  contest      — the item is about who is winning (elections, feuds, negotiations
                 as sport, leadership races)
  neither      — off-lens entirely

NOVELTY 0–3: 3 = a new fact about the world; 0 = another episode of an ongoing story
with nothing structurally new.

MECHANISM: name the causal machinery in ≤12 words, e.g. "export discipline via credit
allocation", "deposit flight from uninsured banks", "acreage-reduction subsidy never
unwound". Null if there is none.

Bias notes: East Asia, Britain, and Europe are of high interest but do NOT inflate FIT
for them; the lens is the only criterion. A US item scores exactly as any other. Ignore
the source's own framing of importance.
