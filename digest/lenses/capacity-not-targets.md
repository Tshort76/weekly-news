LENS: capacity built, not targets announced.

Score FIT 0–3 on whether the item describes a change in physical or institutional
capacity: what was built, connected, closed, repriced, or made buildable.

3 — Capacity changed, or the rule that governs it did. Examples: generation or storage
    connected, or retired, at a scale that moves a system; a grid or interconnection
    rule that releases or blocks capacity; an auction or tariff design change that
    alters who can build; a supply chain for a critical input reroutes for durable
    reasons; a permitting regime created or revoked; a cost crossing the level where a
    technology gets built without subsidy.
2 — Meaningful movement inside the existing system: a large project reaching final
    investment decision, an industrial policy with real money and a named mechanism, a
    regulator changing what a utility may recover.
1 — Interesting but mostly pledges: a net-zero target, a summit communiqué, a corporate
    commitment, a modelling study of a scenario — UNLESS the item explains what changes
    physically or legally as a result.
0 — Personalities, activism as spectacle, opinion pieces without a mechanism,
    predictions, prizes, greenwashing rows.

KIND:
  capacity — the item is about what exists or is now buildable (plant, grid, rules,
             costs)
  pledge   — the item is about what was promised (targets, communiqués, commitments)
  neither  — off-lens entirely

NOVELTY 0–3: 3 = a new fact about the world; 0 = another episode of an ongoing story
with nothing structurally new.

MECHANISM: name the causal machinery in ≤12 words, e.g. "interconnection queue reform
releasing stalled capacity", "auction design shifting who can bid", "grid constraint
priced into dispatch". Null if there is none.

Bias notes: A megawatt counts the same wherever it is. Ignore the source's own framing
of importance.
