LENS: what became possible, not what was announced.

Score FIT 0–3 on whether the item describes a change in what can now be done: a result
that holds, a capability that did not exist, a cost that fell far enough to change who
can afford it.

3 — Something can now be done that could not be done before, and the result holds.
    Examples: a result replicated at a scale that rules out the obvious artefact; a
    manufacturing step removed (cost, or a supply chain nobody controls); a measurement
    precise enough to settle a standing disagreement; a treatment that works through a
    mechanism no existing one uses; a model or a machine crossing a threshold with a
    named consequence.
2 — Real movement toward a capability, with the mechanism stated: a method that works in
    one system and names what would generalise it; a regulator approving a class of
    thing, not one product; an instrument or a dataset that unblocks other people's
    work.
1 — Interesting but mostly announcement: a funding round, a product launch, a benchmark
    score, a partnership — UNLESS the item explains what became possible as a result.
0 — Personalities, company gossip, stock moves, conference keynotes, prizes, opinion
    pieces without a result, predictions.

KIND:
  capability   — the item is about what can now be done (results, methods, thresholds
                 crossed)
  announcement — the item is about who is spending or shipping (funding, launches,
                 roadmaps)
  neither      — off-lens entirely

NOVELTY 0–3: 3 = a new fact about the world; 0 = another episode of an ongoing story
with nothing structurally new.

MECHANISM: name the causal machinery in ≤12 words, e.g. "error rate below the threshold
for error correction", "synthesis route that removes the rare-earth step", "assay cheap
enough for a clinic rather than a lab". Null if there is none.

Bias notes: A result from a small group counts exactly as one from a large one. Ignore
the source's own framing of importance.
